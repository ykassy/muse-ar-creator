TALK TO MUSE, UNLOCK AR

MOSHI MOSHI

Say a theme in Muse, and an AR appears. This connector composes the AR on the spot with the Meta Model API — Muse Spark designs the title, text, color and particle effect, Muse Image generates the artwork — then publishes it as an app-less WebAR page you can open on any phone, HACKAR:S-style.

  1. Connect MOSHI MOSHI in the Muse app.
  2. Say "I want to make an AR" and give a one-word theme.
  3. Open the published link on your phone — no app needed.
  4. Get a certificate page for the AR you created.

Built by HACK IT INC. for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (Cerebral Valley, Oct 26 – Nov 4, 2026).
