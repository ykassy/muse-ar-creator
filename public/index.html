SAY HELLO TO YOUR CHARACTERS

MOSHI MOSHI

Talk to Muse and a character you can touch and talk to is born on HACKAR:S, our production WebAR platform. Three ways to try it:

1. Meet the cloud (phone, no app)

Scan the code, allow the camera and microphone, aim at the floor. Pinch it to pick it up, press Talk and ask it to jump, show your palm for its spell.

hackar-s.com/slam-ar/350e9339…

On a laptop: open it in our AR emulator and press Place Content.

2. Make your own (Muse)

Add https://hackathon-ar-creator.vercel.app/mcp as a custom connector, then say “Moshi Moshi” and give a theme or a photo of a sketch. A fresh character takes about a minute to compose, then you get its hackar-s.com link.

3. Play the stamp rally

With the connector added, say Join DRIZZLY-7913 in Muse, tell it where you are, and answer the cloud’s questions. Without Muse: the same rally on the web.

Built by HACK IT INC. for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (Cerebral Valley). Source code. The connector calls the Meta Model API; each fresh character uses a small amount of paid credit, and paid calls have rate limits.
